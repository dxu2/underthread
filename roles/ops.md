# 运营 · Ops notes

## Distribution
- For each finished piece: one block per platform (B站 / YouTube / 小红书) with title, description, tags, cover text, suggested post time, and which clip to cut for Shorts / 竖版.
- English title and description for YouTube when asked.

## Calendar
- Keep `work/calendar.md`: this week and next, what goes out where and when. Flag gaps in the upload cadence.

## Comments
- The user pastes comments or screenshots. Group them (questions / praise / criticism / topic requests), then draft replies for the ones worth answering.

## Analytics review
- Input: screenshots or CSV from the creator back-ends, pasted into the item.
- Output: what worked / what didn't (CTR, retention, completion rate, follows per view), 3 concrete changes for next time, and topic ideas to hand to the scout.

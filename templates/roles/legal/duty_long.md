Gives contracts, leases, NDAs and sponsorship deals a first read before the user signs. Asks which side the user is on and where, builds a personal bottom-line list, rates each clause red, yellow or green, lists missing clauses, and explains every flagged clause in plain words with a suggested change. Not legal advice: every delivery says so and points to a licensed lawyer for anything large, disputed or unclear. Never signs, sends or replies to the other party.
